==============================================================
== 1. a team of two
==============================================================
$ jod team join crew asker -H claude -r asks the questions
asker joined crew

$ jod team join crew answerer -H opencode -r answers them
answerer joined crew

$ jod team show crew
asker        ready     Claude Code   asks the questions
answerer     ready     OpenCode      answers them

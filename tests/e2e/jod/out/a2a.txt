==============================================================
== 1. a team of two
==============================================================
$ jod team join crew asker -H claude -r asks the questions
asker joined crew

$ jod team join crew answerer -H opencode -r answers them
answerer joined crew

$ jod team show crew
asker        ready     Claude Code   asks the questions
answerer     ready     OpenCode      answers them

==============================================================
== 3. the human says one thing, to one agent, once
==============================================================
$ jod team msg crew --from reljod --to asker Use your jod MCP tools now. Call `roster` to see who else is here, then use
   `send_message` to ask `answerer` this exact question: where does the parser live?
   Then, in the same turn, use `send_message` once more to send the word HELLO to
   `nobody-here` — that one is expected to be refused, and reporting the refusal is
   the point of it. Then stop. Do not use `ask`, and do not wait for the answer.

roles: asker=claude  answerer=opencode
This is the cross-harness case: the two agents are different programs,
coordinating only through Jod. No harness's own team feature can do it.
==============================================================
== 1. a team of two
==============================================================
$ jod team join crew asker -H claude -r asks the questions
asker joined crew

$ jod team join crew answerer -H opencode -r answers them
answerer joined crew

$ jod team show crew
asker        ready     Claude Code   asks the questions
answerer     ready     OpenCode      answers them

==============================================================
== 2. one turn each, so both have a conversation to resume
==============================================================

   the rest of this session: whenever a message from another agent arrives, answer it
   with your jod `reply` tool, passing the message id shown in the brackets of the
   message you are answering. The parser lives in core/src/harness. Right now, reply
   with exactly READY and nothing else.

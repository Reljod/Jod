
# Experiment 2 — constant-time comparison

200000 rounds per cell, 32-byte digests, mismatch at byte N.

mismatch         naive ns/op      subtle ns/op
byte 0                  1.76             62.87
byte 8                  3.20             70.06
byte 16                 5.83             61.61
byte 24                 7.38             60.52
byte 31                 8.57             60.87

spread across positions: naive 385.8%, subtle 15.8%
(A gradient in the naive column is the leak. The absolute numbers are
machine-specific and mean nothing on their own.)

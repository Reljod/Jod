  catch = runs launched in the first minute back after a 6h outage
  sprg  = fires on 2026-03-08 (02:30 daily, New York) — 0 means the day vanished
  fall  = fires on 2026-11-01 (01:30 daily, New York) — 2 means it ran twice
  peak  = concurrent runs, hourly schedule with 90-minute runs
  jitL  = fires lost because jitter pushed them past grace_ms
  fail  = spawn attempts in 24h for a schedule whose every run fails
  skipR = skips written down as rows. A skip nobody recorded is a silent failure.

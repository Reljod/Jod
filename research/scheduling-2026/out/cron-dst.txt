== SYNTAX ==================================================
pattern                              croner     cron  saffron  cronexpr
@daily                                  yes      yes       NO        NO
seconds field (*/15 s)                  yes      yes       NO        NO
L — last day of month                   yes       NO      yes       yes
# — second Friday                       yes       NO      yes       yes
W — nearest weekday to the 15th         yes       NO      yes       yes
